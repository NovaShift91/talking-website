Questions providers ask

Or just ask the assistant — it knows all of this and more.

Who is Harper for?

Broadband and fiber providers of any size, especially community-focused ones without a big in-house marketing team. Rural telcos, electric co-ops, municipal networks, and small cable or wireless ISPs are the core.

Can I customize the content?

Yes. Most assets brand in a single click, then you can keep editing inside Harper. Need something beyond that? The team handles additional customizations for subscribers at no cost.

Is there a contract?

No setup fee. Join at a monthly or annual rate. Cancel within 24 hours of payment for a full refund.

What is Harper Direct?

Our direct mail service. We print, address, and mail 6x9 color postcards using Harper creative, with clear upfront pricing and savings as you mail more. Minimum is 500 pieces per mailing.

Who owns Harper?

Harper is part of ISPN Network Services, which acquired the platform along with DxTEL's marketing services in July 2025.
